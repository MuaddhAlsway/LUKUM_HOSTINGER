SCROLLING BEHAVIOR
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Menu Height Calculation:
  max-height = 100vh - 60px (header height)
  
Example on iPhone 12 (844px height):
  Menu max-height = 844px - 60px = 784px
  
If menu items exceed 784px:
  • Scrollbar appears
  • Smooth scrolling enabled
  • Momentum scrolling on iOS
  • Touch-friendly
