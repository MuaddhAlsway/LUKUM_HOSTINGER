================================================================================
RESPONSIVE DESIGN PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

1. MOBILE-FIRST APPROACH
   Start with mobile (< 480px) and enhance for larger screens

2. PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT
   Add features as screen size increases

3. BREAKPOINT STRATEGY
   - 480px: Small phones
   - 768px: Tablets and large phones
   - 820px: Medium tablets (NEW)
   - 1024px: Large tablets and small desktops
   - 1200px+: Full desktop

4. CONTENT PRIORITY
   Show most important content first on mobile

5. TOUCH-FRIENDLY
   Minimum 44px touch targets on mobile

6. PERFORMANCE
   Optimize for each breakpoint

7. ACCESSIBILITY
   Maintain accessibility at all sizes
