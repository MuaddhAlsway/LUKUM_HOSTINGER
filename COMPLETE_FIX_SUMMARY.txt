COMPLETE FIX SUMMARY - Blog, Press, Event Pages
================================================================================

ISSUES REPORTED:
❌ event.php?id=72 shows "Event not found"
❌ blog.php shows no data (blank grid)
❌ press.php shows "Loading press releases..." (never loads)

ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED:
The pages were waiting for LanguageManager to load before fetching data.
This caused delays and sometimes prevented data from loading at all.

SOLUTION APPLIED:
Removed LanguageManager dependency check from initialization functions.
Pages now load data immediately when DOM is ready.
