TECHNICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

WHY FIXED POSITIONING WORKS:

Old (Broken):
   <header z-index: 1000>
     <nav position: relative>
       <item position: relative>
         <dropdown position: absolute>

   Problem: Dropdown positioned relative to nav (relative element)
   Result: Breaks layout when nav is on small screen

New (Fixed):
   <header z-index: 1000>
     <nav position: static>
       <item position: static>
         <dropdown position: fixed>

   Benefit: Dropdown positioned relative to viewport
   Result: Never breaks layout, always floats correctly

WHY OPACITY INSTEAD OF TRANSFORM:

Old: transform: scaleY(0.95) → scaleY(1)
   - Creates rendering layer
   - Causes reflow
   - Janky on slow devices

New: opacity: 0 → 1
   - Simple property change
   - No layer creation
   - Smooth 60fps
