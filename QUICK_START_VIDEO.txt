saved to the database. This has now been fixed!

===================================
HOW TO ADD A VIDEO TO AN EXHIBITION
===================================

1. Go to your admin panel at: https://yourdomain.com/admin/
2. Click on "Exhibitions" in the sidebar
3. Click "Add New Exhibition" button
4. Fill in the exhibition details:
   - Title (English and Arabic)
   - Description
   - Location
   - Date & Time
   - Cover Image
5. SCROLL DOWN to "Event Video (Optional)" section
6. Paste your video URL in the field:
   ✓ YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
   ✓ YouTube: https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID
   ✓ Vimeo: https://vimeo.com/VIDEO_ID
7. (Optional) Add gallery images
8. Click "Create Exhibition" button

EXAMPLE VIDEO URLS:
─────────────────
✓ YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JH3zXmuFARw
✓ YouTube: https://youtu.be/JH3zXmuFARw
✓ Vimeo: https://vimeo.com/76979871

AFTER CREATING:
──────────────
1. Video is now saved to database
2. Go to your main website
3. Navigate to "Spaces" page
4. Click on a past exhibition
5. Scroll down to "Event Video" section
6. Video should play automatically!

HOW TO VERIFY IT'S WORKING:
──────────────────────────
While adding/editing an exhibition:

1. Open Browser Developer Tools: Press F12
2. Click on "Console" tab
3. Paste video URL
4. Click "Create Exhibition" button
5. In console, you should see:
   ✓ "Element found: true"
   ✓ "Element value: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=..."
   ✓ "event_video value from form: https://..."

If you see these messages, the video is being captured correctly!

IF VIDEO DOESN'T APPEAR:
───────────────────────
1. Make sure the exhibition date is in the PAST
   (Exhibitions require past dates for security)

2. Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)

3. Try this test URL: https://youtu.be/dQw4w9WgXcQ
   If this works, your video URL format might be wrong

4. Check browser console (F12) for errors

EDITING EXISTING EXHIBITIONS:
────────────────────────────
1. Go to Admin → Exhibitions
2. Click the exhibition to edit
3. Scroll to "Event Video (Optional)"
4. Paste video URL
5. Click "Update Exhibition"
6. Video will be saved!

TO REMOVE A VIDEO:
─────────────────
1. Go to edit exhibition
2. Clear the video URL field (delete the URL)
3. Click "Update Exhibition"
4. Video URL will be removed

SUPPORTED VIDEO PLATFORMS:
─────────────────────────
✓ YouTube (youtube.com and youtu.be)
✓ Vimeo (vimeo.com)
✗ Other platforms not supported yet

TIPS:
────
• Use the short YouTube URL (youtu.be) - it's cleaner
• Always use the direct video link, not playlist or channel
• Video must be publicly accessible
• Video will appear in "Event Video" section on event detail page
• Videos are responsive and work on mobile devices

TROUBLESHOOTING:
───────────────
Problem: Video URL field won't accept my URL
Solution: Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete) and reload page

Problem: Video saves but doesn't appear on website
Solution: 1) Check exhibition date is in past
         2) Refresh website with Ctrl+F5
         3) Try accessing the event page directly

Problem: Console shows "Element found: false"
Solution: This means the form isn't loaded properly.
         Refresh page and try again.
