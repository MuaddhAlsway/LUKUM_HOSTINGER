     • No srcset/sizes attributes
     • Impact: Mobile users download 3-4x larger images

     • api-cache-manager.js + api-cache-manager-enhanced.js (both loaded)
     • Multiple button styles in different files
     • Multiple typography styles duplicated

     • Savings: 30 KB

     • Not responsive
